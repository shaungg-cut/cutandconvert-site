The problem

Most Google accounts are just catching people who already know you.

Branded search and retargeting make the dashboard look great, but they mostly take credit for sales you would have gotten anyway. Real growth comes from reaching people who have never heard of you.

We build Google and YouTube around new-customer acquisition, then prove it with third-party attribution and incrementality testing, so you know what each dollar actually adds.
