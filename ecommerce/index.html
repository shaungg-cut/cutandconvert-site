Product feed optimization

On Shopping, your product feed is your ad.

Shopping and Performance Max don't run on keywords. Google reads your product feed to decide which searches your products show up for, and how they look when they do. A thin or messy feed caps your reach before a single bid is placed.

We rebuild feeds in Merchant Center and tools like DataFeedWatch so every product carries the details shoppers search for.

The big three, plus the category most brands skip

These four fields do most of the work in deciding when your products show and whether they get the click.

01

Titles

The most important field in the feed. We front-load the words shoppers actually type: brand, product type and the attributes that matter in your category, like size, color, material or who it's for.

02

Images

The first thing a shopper sees. Clean, compliant main images that stand out in a grid of competitors, plus additional and lifestyle images. We test variants to find the one that earns the click.

03

Descriptions

Where context lives. We lead with what the product is and who it's for, then cover materials, features and use cases in the language customers use, without keyword stuffing.

04

Google product category

We map every product to the most specific category in Google's taxonomy so it's matched to the right searches, and use product types and custom labels to structure campaigns and bidding.
