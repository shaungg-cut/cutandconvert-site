About

Media buyers by trade. Filmmakers at heart.

Cut & Convert is a San Diego agency that builds Google and YouTube campaigns like strategists and cuts the creative like filmmakers.

The story

Cut & Convert was founded by Shaun Malinao. For 9+ years he has run paid media for some of the fastest-growing DTC brands in the country, across Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Bing and connected TV. He has managed $500M+ in ad spend, scaled a telehealth brand from $40K to over $300K a day while holding CPA, and grew a subscription supplement brand from $500 a day to $30K a day before it was acquired by Unilever.

The creative side started early. Shaun's dad owned a photography and sign company, so he grew up around design, and he started classical piano at 9. He has worked as a live video director at a major San Diego theme park, and runs Rolling Path Studios, a wedding film company here in San Diego, where one of his highlight films has passed 275,000 views.

Over the years he kept seeing the same gap: media buyers who don't understand creative, and creatives who don't understand performance. Cut & Convert is built to close it. The team planning your campaigns is the same team that knows how to cut a video that makes someone stop, watch and buy.
