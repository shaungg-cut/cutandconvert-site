About

Media buyer by trade. Filmmaker at heart.

I'm Shaun Malinao, the founder of Cut & Convert. I build Google and YouTube campaigns like a strategist and cut the creative like a filmmaker.

The story

For 9+ years I've run paid media for some of the fastest-growing DTC brands in the country, across Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Bing and connected TV. I've managed $500M+ in ad spend, scaled a telehealth brand from $40K to over $300K a day while holding CPA, and grew a subscription supplement brand from $500 a day to $30K a day before it was acquired by Unilever.

The creative side started early. My dad owned a photography and sign company, so I grew up around design, and I started classical piano at 9. I've worked as a live video director at a major San Diego theme park, and I run Rolling Path Studios, a wedding film company here in San Diego, where one of my highlight films has passed 275,000 views.

Over the years I kept seeing the same gap: media buyers who don't understand creative, and creatives who don't understand performance. Cut & Convert is built to close it. The person planning your campaigns is the same person who knows how to cut a video that makes someone stop, watch and buy.
