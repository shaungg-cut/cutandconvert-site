What works on Meta and TikTok doesn't always work on YouTube.

YouTube has its own audience, formats and rules. Dropping your best Reels or TikToks into a YouTube campaign usually leaves performance on the table.

The skip button

In-stream viewers can skip after 5 seconds. Your hook, brand and reason to keep watching all have to land before then.

Sound on, lean back

People watch YouTube with the sound on, often on a TV or laptop. Voiceover, music and pacing carry more weight than on a muted social feed.

Horizontal screens

In-stream and connected TV are 16:9. Vertical video shows up small, boxed in and easy to ignore.

Room to tell a story

Viewers who stay will watch longer. 30 to 90 second cuts can demo the product, answer objections and build trust.

Search intent

People use YouTube to research before they buy, so reviews, demos and comparisons perform.

Shorts is its own format

Shorts is vertical and fast, but the audience and placements differ from TikTok. We cut versions specifically for it.
